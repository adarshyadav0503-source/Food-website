# Savoria - World Kitchen 🍽️

A modern, responsive food and restaurant website showcasing authentic culinary creations from **India 🇮🇳**, **China 🇨🇳**, and **Italy 🇮🇹**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **36 Signature Dishes with Complete Home Cooking Recipes**:
   - **Indian Gastronomy (12 dishes)**:
     - *Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)*, *Paneer Tikka Masala*, *Royal Awadhi Dum Biryani*, *Dal Makhani Grand Heritage*
     - *Tandoori Garlic Butter Naan*, *Delhi Street Samosa Chaat*, *South Indian Masala Dosa*, *Warm Saffron Gulab Jamun*
     - *Kashmiri Mutton Rogan Josh*, *Delhi Chole Bhature Special*, *Shahi Malai Kofta in Velvety Gravy*, *Charcoal Murgh Tikka Kebab*
   - **Chinese Gastronomy (12 dishes)**:
     - *Sichuan Kung Pao Chicken*, *Crystal Steamed Dim Sum Platter*, *Szechuan Dan Dan Noodles*, *Claypot Mapo Tofu*
     - *Golden Crispy Vegetable Spring Rolls*, *Cantonese Sweet & Sour Crispy Chicken*, *Imperial Hot and Sour Broth*, *Yangzhou Wok Fried Rice*
     - *Crispy Imperial Peking Duck Rolls*, *General Tso's Golden Crispy Chicken*, *Hong Kong Crispy Vegetable Chow Mein*, *Chilled Mango Pomelo Sago Delight*
   - **Italian Gastronomy (12 dishes)**:
     - *Neapolitan Margherita Pizza D.O.C.*, *Black Truffle Fettuccine Alfredo*, *Classic Lasagna Alla Bolognese*, *Wild Porcini Risotto ai Funghi*
     - *Bruschetta Rustica al Pomodoro*, *Artisanal Tiramisù al Mascarpone*, *Authentic Spaghetti alla Carbonara*, *Vanilla Bean Panna Cotta con Frutti di Bosco*
     - *Quattro Formaggi Woodfired Pizza*, *Penne all'Arrabbiata Tradizionale*, *Handmade Potato Gnocchi al Pesto Genovese*, *Artisanal Cannoli Siciliani*

2. **Step-by-Step "How to Make at Home" Masterclass**:
   - Difficulty level, prep & cook times, and portion servings.
   - Recommended kitchen cookware & equipment guides.
   - Measured ingredients with interactive clickable checkboxes (strike through items as you prep).
   - Numbered cooking instructions with pro techniques.
   - Executive Chef secret tips for authentic flavor.
   - Direct "Print Recipe Card" button (`@media print` supported).

3. **Interactive Menu & Multi-Filtering**:
   - Filter by Cuisine (`All 36`, `Indian 12`, `Chinese 12`, `Italian 12`).
   - Filter by Course (`All Courses`, `Starters & Dim Sum`, `Main Courses`, `Breads & Rice`, `Desserts & Sweets`).
   - Dietary Toggles: `All`, `Veg Only 🟢`, `Non-Veg 🔴`.
   - Live Search: Instant filtering by dish title, ingredients, or cuisine.
   - Sorting: Popularity, Rating, Price (Low to High / High to Low).

4. **Slide-Over Shopping Cart & Checkout**:
   - Real-time cart drawer with quantity steppers (`+` / `-`).
   - Free delivery progress indicator (Free gourmet delivery above ₹500).
   - Promo discount coupon codes:
     - `FEAST20` - 20% discount on entire order.
     - `FIRSTBITE` - Flat ₹100 discount on orders above ₹300.
     - `CHEF50` - ₹50 discount on orders above ₹250.
   - Complete checkout with address input and payment methods (UPI/QR, Card, Cash on Delivery).
   - Order tracking simulation with live status updates (*Confirmed* ➔ *Cooking* ➔ *On the Way* ➔ *Delivered*).

5. **Table Reservation System**:
   - Online booking form for guests, dates, times, and cuisine preferences.
   - Instant confirmation modal with booking details.

6. **Design & UX Polish**:
   - Dark / Light mode toggle with local storage persistence.
   - Responsive design crafted for smartphones, tablets, and desktops.
   - Non-intrusive floating toast notifications for user actions.

## 🚀 How to Run

### Option 1: Direct in Browser (Zero Installation)
Simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer, or open it with Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge / Firefox.

### Option 2: Local Web Server
You can also run a local server using Node:
```powershell
npm.cmd start
```
or
```powershell
npx.cmd serve .
```
Then open the provided `http://localhost:...` URL in your browser.
